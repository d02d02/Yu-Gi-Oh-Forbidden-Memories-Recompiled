# Kuriboh and the HD mod (decided 2026-10-10; compacted 2026-10-13)

Question: keep the 722 vanilla ids (A) or move the mod's 18 new cards past 722 (B)? **Decided with the user: A (keep the ids) + a second small HD mod `kuriboh-hd`.**

## Data (base Kuriboh disc vs retail vs `assets-hd`)
`assets-hd` has HD art + thumbnail for all 722 retail cards. Kuriboh changes 38 slots: **18 genuinely different cards** (their retail-slot HD picture is the wrong card): 338 Multiply (was Mooyan Curry), 343 Kuriboh Multiplied (Sparks), 393 Berserk Dragon (Zone Eater), 394 Dragon Master Knight (Steel Scorpion), 402 Red-Eyes B. Metal Dragon (Monster Eye), 484 Ryu-Ran (Ameba), 499 Five-Headed Dragon (Kattapillar), 666 Dragon Revival Ritual, 674 Barrel Dragon, 676 Dark Sage, 677 Toon World, 681 Mirror Force (House of Adhesive Tape), 682 Manga Ryu-Ran (Eatgaboon), 683 Toon Summoned Skull (Bear Trap), 684 Blue-Eyes Toon Dragon (Invisible Wire), 693 Exodia Necross (Contruct of Mask), 695 Contract with Exodia, 699 A Deal with Dark Ruler; 16 ritual spells turned "equip" (same picture, redrawn plate, blue frame kept by a `frame` entry); 3 redrawn plates only (Sanga of the Thunder, Kazejin, Suijin); 1 text artifact (Kuwagata alpha).

## Why A
Every id-bearing table (2664 fusions, pools, extra duelists' decks, unique drops, terrain lists, passwords, equips, dialogue, AI scripts in opponent records) and the original disc's save (chest by these ids) use the slots; the six attack traps are engine-fixed at ids 681-686 (Mirror Force + 682-684). B needs an id remap in every table, hiding the replaced retail rows, a `trap_thresholds` entry past 722 (unsupported), and breaks save compatibility; right for a new mod, not for reproducing this one. If ever wanted: an importer step, keep A as the exact mode.

## Built
- Two packs naming the same image (offset, size, depth, palette) resolve by load order: `kuriboh` declares `after: ["forbidden-memories-hd", ...]`. **Mod id trap:** folder `mods/assets-hd` holds the mod whose id is `forbidden-memories-hd`; settings and `after`/`requires` use the id (`mod.forbidden-memories-hd.full_bleed=1` = anime frame; earlier `assets-hd` names matched nothing).
- `tools/pc/kuriboh/make_hd.py` builds `kuriboh-hd` (requires `kuriboh`): 36 entries (18 pictures + 18 thumbnails) at 4x (Lanczos + light unsharp); `--portraits` enlarges the 4 portraits (Yami Bakura, Kaiba, Heishin 2nd, Duel Master K); `--sources <folder>` fits per-card pictures `<slot>.png` (crop to 102:96 from the middle, a little high; 408x384; thumbnail from the game's crop; no file = Lanczos). `build_kuriboh.py <disc> --game <retail> [--sources <folder>] --install` = import + HD layer + install into `disc.user_dir()` mods.
- Name plates need nothing: with HD text on the port redraws each card's plate crisp from its name (`hd_text.c` `HdText_Title`, `CardArt_TitlePicture`; checked Berserk Dragon at 4x).
- Artwork: `fetch_artwork.py tmp/kuriboh/hd-source` asks Yugipedia for 512x512 Master Duel art (`<Card>-MADU-EN-VG-artwork.png`) per slot: 16 of 18 found (343, 666 have no real card). Not committed (Konami art, the user's own use).
- AI candidates: `ai_upscale.py <imported mod> <out>` writes `raw/` (realesrgan-x4plus-anime), `mix/` (60% model + Lanczos, back-projected), `project/` (the HD mod's own method, `tools/pc/hd_screen_pack.py`: x4plus on a mirror-padded picture, 60/40 Lanczos, 6 back-projection rounds), `net_ai/` (real artwork fitted to 2x frame size, anime model x4, reduced to 408x384); `compare_art.py` makes `compare_1..3.png`. Tool: realesrgan-ncnn-vulkan (20220424) in `tmp/pc/tools/realesrgan/` (shared junction; Intel Iris Xe via Vulkan, ~1.5 s a picture; absolute Windows paths + `-m <models dir>`). How `assets-hd` got its quality (`notes/image-remaster.md`): redrawn images, not a blanket AI upscale ("filtered, not redrawn").
- **Chosen by the user:** real artwork through the anime model for 16 cards (`net_ai/`), the anime model on the mod's own art for 343 and 666 (`raw/`), collected in `tmp/kuriboh/hd-chosen/`. Checked at 2x with the anime frame: new monster = gold frame with the layer's picture, new spell/ritual-like equips = green frame.
